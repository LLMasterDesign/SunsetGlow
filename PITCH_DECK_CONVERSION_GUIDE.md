# 🎨 PITCH DECK CONVERSION GUIDE
## Turn Your Markdown Deck into a Visual Presentation

**Current File:** `FRIENDS_FAMILY_PITCH_DECK.md`  
**Goal:** Convert to PowerPoint/PDF for easy sharing  
**Time Required:** 30–60 minutes  
**Cost:** $0 (using free tools)  

---

## 🚀 FASTEST METHOD: Canva (Recommended)

### Step 1: Create Free Canva Account
1. Go to [canva.com](https://www.canva.com)
2. Sign up (free account is fine)
3. Click "Create a design" → "Presentation"

### Step 2: Choose a Template
Search for: **"Startup Pitch Deck"** or **"Business Proposal"**  
Recommended templates:
- "Modern Startup Pitch Deck" (clean, professional)
- "Minimalist Business Presentation" (simple, text-focused)
- "Bold Investor Pitch" (visual, high-impact)

### Step 3: Customize with Your Content
Copy-paste text from `FRIENDS_FAMILY_PITCH_DECK.md` into slides:

**Slide 1:** Title slide  
- Text: "Sunset Glow Holiday Lighting — Friends & Family Investment Opportunity"
- Subtitle: "Presented by [Your Name] | Oct 15, 2025"
- Background: Sunset gradient or holiday lights photo

**Slide 2:** The Problem  
- Headline: "Every November, Homeowners Face the Same Dilemma"
- Bullet points: DIY hassle, expensive chains, cheap handymen
- Visual: Photo of tangled lights or person on ladder (stock photo from Canva library)

**Slide 3:** The Solution  
- Headline: "Sunset Glow Holiday Lighting"
- Tagline: "Turnkey Professional Installations • Veteran-Owned • Princeton, TX"
- Bullet points: What we offer, pricing tiers
- Visual: Before/after home photos (use your website images OR stock photos)

**Continue for all 15 slides...**

### Step 4: Add Visuals (Stock Photos)
Canva has millions of free stock photos. Search for:
- "holiday lights"
- "christmas house"
- "ladder safety"
- "family home"
- "veteran"
- "handshake" (for investor slides)
- "money growth" (for financial projections)

### Step 5: Brand Colors
Use Sunset Glow palette:
- **Sunset Gold:** #F8C36A
- **Amber:** #F39A54
- **Warm Red:** #D9663A
- **Dark Ink:** #171717
- **Off White:** #FAFAFA

(Click "Brand Kit" in Canva → add these colors → apply to all slides)

### Step 6: Export
1. Click "Share" → "Download"
2. Choose format:
   - **PDF** (best for email/text)
   - **PowerPoint** (if you want to edit further)
   - **PNG** (individual slide images for social media)

### Step 7: Share
- **Email:** Attach PDF to investor outreach emails
- **Text:** Upload PDF to Google Drive → share link via text
- **Print:** Print slides as handouts for in-person meetings (1–2 pages per sheet)

**Total Time:** 30–45 minutes

## 📄 SIMPLEST METHOD: PDF from Markdown

If you want to skip visual design and just convert the markdown directly:

### Option 1: Use Pandoc (Command Line)
1. Install Pandoc: [pandoc.org](https://pandoc.org)
2. Open Terminal (Mac) or Command Prompt (Windows)
3. Navigate to folder with markdown file:
   ```
   cd /workspace
   ```
4. Convert to PDF:
   ```
   pandoc FRIENDS_FAMILY_PITCH_DECK.md -o pitch_deck.pdf
   ```

**Total Time:** 5 minutes (if you're comfortable with command line)

### Option 2: Online Markdown → PDF Converter
1. Go to [markdown-pdf.com](https://www.markdown-pdf.com) (or similar free tool)
2. Upload `FRIENDS_FAMILY_PITCH_DECK.md`
3. Click "Convert"
4. Download PDF

**Total Time:** 2 minutes

For in-person coffee meetings:

3. Export as PDF

## ✅ FINAL CHECKLIST

Before sharing your pitch deck with investors:

- [ ] All slides have visuals (no text-only slides)
- [ ] Your contact info is on Slide 1 AND Slide 14
- [ ] Financial projections are accurate (double-check math)
- [ ] You've tested the PDF on your phone (is text readable?)
- [ ] You've spell-checked everything (typos kill credibility)
- [ ] You've shown it to 1 trusted friend (get feedback)
- [ ] You feel confident explaining every slide (practice out loud)
