# SGL Customer Intake Form

**Location:** `SGL.Website/intake.html`

**Purpose:** Detailed customer onboarding form for clients who have accepted quotes. Collects installation preferences, property access details, scheduling, and service preferences.

**Features:**
- Netlify Forms integration (no backend needed)
- All submissions stored in Netlify dashboard
- Can be exported or connected to CRM via Zapier
- Accessible at: `https://sunsetglowlighting.com/intake.html`

**Form Sections:**
1. Customer Information (contact details, preferences)
2. Property Information (address, access codes, special features)
3. Service Details (package, colors, add-ons)
4. Installation Scheduling (dates, time preferences)
5. Payment Information (method, billing)
6. Emergency Contact
7. Additional Notes & Referral Source
8. Consent & Agreement

**Netlify Integration:**
- Form submissions appear in Netlify dashboard under "Forms"
- Can export to CSV or connect to CRMs like HubSpot, Salesforce via Zapier
- Email notifications can be configured in Netlify settings
