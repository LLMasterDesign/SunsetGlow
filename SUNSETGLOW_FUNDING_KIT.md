<a name="risk--mitigation-plan"></a>
## ⚠️ RISK & MITIGATION PLAN

### Risk 1: Weather Delays (Rain, Ice, Wind)

**Impact:** Install delays → revenue backlog → customer dissatisfaction  
**Probability:** Moderate (Texas weather is generally mild Sept–Dec, but unpredictable)  
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ **Weather buffer in scheduling:** Book 80% of available days (leave 20% for rescheduling)
- ✅ **Contract clause:** "Installations subject to safe weather conditions; delays communicated 24 hrs in advance"
- ✅ **Indoor prep work:** Pre-assemble light kits during bad weather (maximizes efficiency when skies clear)

---

### Risk 2: Crew Turnover / No-Shows

**Impact:** Missed installs → refunds → reputation damage  
**Probability:** Moderate (seasonal labor is notoriously unreliable)  
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ **Competitive pay structure:** 20%–40% of labor pool (vs. flat $15/hr) = performance incentive
- ✅ **Raise reviews every 20–30 homes:** Quick skill recognition = retention
- ✅ **Backup crew roster:** 1–2 on-call installers (college students, retirees) for emergencies
- ✅ **Owner as safety valve:** Founder installs if crew fails (controlled scaling prevents over-commitment)

---

### Risk 3: Customer Acquisition Cost Exceeds Projections

**Impact:** $1,000 ad spend yields 2 jobs instead of 5 → negative ROI  
**Probability:** Low-Moderate (early-stage marketing often underperforms until optimized)  
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ **Organic channels first:** Door-to-door, yard signs, local Facebook groups (near-zero cost)
- ✅ **Referral program:** "Refer 2 friends, get $100 credit" = customer-funded marketing
- ✅ **Hyper-local targeting:** Focus on 3 zip codes (Princeton, McKinney, Prosper) = lower CPC
- ✅ **Google Ads optimization:** Start with $20/day, A/B test ad copy, pause underperformers

---

### Risk 4: Material Cost Inflation (LED Lights, Clips)

**Impact:** COGS rises 20% → net margin drops from 33% to 25%  
**Probability:** Low (LED prices have stabilized post-COVID; suppliers locked in for 2025)  
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ **Bulk purchasing:** Buy 20 kits upfront (10% volume discount)
- ✅ **Multi-supplier strategy:** 2–3 vendors (avoid single-source dependency)
- ✅ **Price escalation clause:** Contracts allow 10% price adjustment if COGS rise >15%
- ✅ **Leasing model:** We own lights (reused 3+ years) = COGS amortized, not recurring

---

### Risk 5: Liability Incident (Injury, Property Damage)

**Impact:** Lawsuit, insurance claim, reputation damage

### Phase 2: Insurance & Risk Management (Week 1–2)

- [ ] **General Liability Insurance**
  - **Coverage:** $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate
  - **Protects:** Property damage, bodily injury, advertising injury
  - **Cost:** $800–$1,200/year
  - **Providers:** Hiscox, The Hartford, NEXT Insurance
  - **Industry-specific:** "Holiday lighting installation" or "seasonal decorating services"

- [ ] **Workers' Compensation Insurance**
  - **Required:** If 2+ employees in Texas (non-negotiable)
  - **Coverage:** Medical costs, lost wages if crew injured on job
  - **Cost:** $400–$800/year (seasonal workers, low-risk classification)
  - **Providers:** Texas Mutual, State Farm Business

- [ ] **Commercial Auto Insurance** (if using personal vehicle for business)
  - **Coverage:** Business use endorsement or commercial policy
  - **Cost:** +$200–$400/year on top of personal auto
  - **Why:** Personal auto policies often exclude business use (claim denial risk)

- [ ] **Umbrella Policy** (Optional, but recommended)
  - **Coverage:** $1M excess liability (kicks in after general liability maxes out)
  - **Cost:** $300–$500/year
  - **Why:** Extra protection for catastrophic claims (e.g., house fire caused by faulty wiring)
