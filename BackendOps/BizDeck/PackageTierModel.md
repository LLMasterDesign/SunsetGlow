# Package Tier Model — Standardized Inventory Theory

**Theory: Standardize light quantities per tier = consistent inventory + predictable pricing + faster installs**

---

## The Model: 3 Standard Tiers + Custom

### Tier 1: Basic
- **Roofline:** 60 ft of C9 LED lights
- **Mini lights (accents):** 4 strands (50-100 ft total)
- **Target property:** Small homes, simple rooflines, 1-story
- **Install time:** 2.5-3 hours (2-person crew)

### Tier 2: Enhanced (Most Popular)
- **Roofline:** 80 ft of C9 LED lights
- **Mini lights (accents):** 8 strands (100-200 ft total)
- **Extras:** Tree wraps (2-3 small trees), wreaths, garland
- **Target property:** Medium homes, moderate complexity, 1-2 story
- **Install time:** 3.5-4.5 hours (2-3 person crew)

### Tier 3: Premium
- **Roofline:** 120 ft of C9 LED lights
- **Mini lights (accents):** 12 strands (150-300 ft total)
- **Extras:** Extensive tree wraps (5+ trees), pathway lights, custom animations
- **Target property:** Large homes, complex rooflines, 2-story+
- **Install time:** 5-7 hours (3-person crew)

### Tier 4: Custom
- **Flexible:** Any combination beyond standard tiers
- **Billed:** By linear foot + labor hours + specialty items
- **Use case:** Estates, commercial, unique designs

---

## Why This Works

### 1. Inventory Management
- **Predictable stock:** Order lights in bulk (60/80/120 ft kits)
- **Fewer SKUs:** 3 standard sizes = simpler warehouse, faster picking
- **Reusable sets:** Each tier's lights stay together (labeled bins per tier)

### 2. Pricing Consistency
- **Standard COGS per tier:** Know exact material cost upfront
- **Easier quoting:** "Your home fits Tier 2" → pull pre-calc price
- **Less custom math:** Only Custom tier requires detailed estimation

### 3. Faster Installs
- **Crew knows the drill:** "Tier 2 = 80 ft roofline + 8 minis"
- **Pre-staged kits:** Lights loaded on truck by tier
- **Predictable labor hours:** 2.5-3 hrs (Tier 1), 3.5-4.5 hrs (Tier 2), 5-7 hrs (Tier 3)

## Benefits of This Theory

1. **Crew training:** "Tier 2 = 80 ft roofline, 8 minis, 3 trees" becomes muscle memory
2. **Sales simplicity:** Show customer 3 options, upsell within tiers
3. **Inventory forecasting:** Order lights in multiples of 60/80/120 ft
4. **Faster takedown:** Pre-labeled bins by tier = organized storage
5. **Multi-year consistency:** Customer gets same tier next year = no re-quoting
