# Multi-Year Retention Equation (Proprietary)

## The Secret Recipe

**Multi-year contracts = predictable revenue + customer lock-in + margin optimization**

### Equation Components

**Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) with Multi-Year:**
```
CLV_multiyear = (Annual_Revenue × Contract_Years) × (1 - Churn_Rate)
```

**Retention Leverage:**
```
Retention_Rate = 1 - (Discount_% / (1 + Cost_of_Acquisition_saved))
```

**Our 10% Discount Math:**
- Customer saves: 10% off annual price
- We save: ~15-20% on acquisition cost (no re-marketing, no re-sales effort)
- Net margin impact: +5-10% over contract lifetime

### Why It Works

1. **Revenue Certainty**
   - 2-year contract = 2x guaranteed revenue
   - Bankable for loans, investor confidence
   - Predictable cash flow for inventory, labor planning

2. **Cost Savings**
   - No re-acquisition marketing spend
   - Reduced admin overhead (one contract vs. two annual renewals)
   - Storage = upsell opportunity with minimal cost

3. **Customer Psychology**
   - 10% discount feels significant
   - Multi-year commitment = perceived premium service
   - Priority scheduling = exclusivity signal

### Investor/Banker View

**Key Metrics to Showcase:**
- % of customers on multi-year contracts
- Average contract value (ACV) × contract length
- Churn rate comparison: multi-year (5-10%) vs. annual (25-35%)
- LTV:CAC ratio improvement (multi-year: 6:1, annual: 3:1)

### Implementation Notes
- Discount is strategic, not desperate—position as "loyalty reward"
- Offer priority scheduling (low-cost, high-value perk)
- Yearly design refresh keeps engagement high
- Auto-renewal with opt-out = default retention
