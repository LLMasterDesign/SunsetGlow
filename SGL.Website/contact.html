Frequently Asked Questions

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry full liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage. You'll never be held liable for accidents or damage during our work.

How long does installation take?

Most residential installations are completed in one day. Larger properties or Premium packages may require 1-2 days. We'll give you an exact timeline in your estimate.

When should I book?

We recommend booking by early October to secure your preferred install date. Our calendar fills quickly as Thanksgiving approaches.

What about payment?

We require 50% deposit at booking and the remaining 50% upon completion. We accept credit cards, checks, and digital payments. Multi-year contracts receive a 10% discount.

Do you handle takedown and storage?

Yes! Takedown is included in every package. We offer optional off-season storage so you don't have to worry about garage clutter.

What if a light goes out?

All packages include at least one complimentary repair visit. Enhanced and Premium packages include unlimited repairs throughout the season.
