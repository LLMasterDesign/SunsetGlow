About Sunset Glow Lighting

From Orange County to Lake Norman to Texas, a decade of making homes glow.

Our Story

Sunset Glow Lighting was founded by a U.S. military veteran who believed that the precision, discipline, and care learned in service could transform the seasonal lighting industry.

After years of watching homeowners struggle with tangled strands, unsafe ladder work, and unreliable contractors, we set out to build a company that treats every home like our own—with respect, safety, and attention to detail.

From our first install in Orange County to establishing roots in Princeton, Texas, we've brought clean work, safe installs, and friendly service to hundreds of families. We're proud to serve our community with the same values that guided our military service: integrity, excellence, and commitment.
