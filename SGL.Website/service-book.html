What We Do

From initial consultation to final removal, we handle every detail so you can focus on enjoying the magic.

🎨

Design & Consultation

On-site or virtual consultation to understand your vision, assess your property, and create a custom lighting plan that perfectly complements your home's architecture.

⚡

Professional Installation

Our insured, experienced team installs commercial-grade LED lights with precision and care. Clean lines, secure clips, and attention to every detail.

🔧

Maintenance & Support

We keep your display shining bright all season long. Enhanced and Premium packages include unlimited repair visits, while Essential includes one complimentary visit.

📦

Seasonal Takedown

Safe, careful removal after the season ends. We schedule takedowns to minimize disruption and preserve all hardware for next year's installation.

🏠

Off-Season Storage

Optional storage service to keep your garage clutter-free. We organize and safely store your lights until next season, ready for quick installation.

💎

Multi-Year Benefits

Commit to 2+ years and enjoy 10% savings, priority scheduling, and annual design refresh consultations to keep your display fresh and exciting.
