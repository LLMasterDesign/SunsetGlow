### Risk 1: Weather Delays → Revenue Backlog
**Mitigation:** Book 80% of available days (20% buffer for rescheduling)

### Risk 2: Crew Turnover / No-Shows
**Mitigation:** Competitive pay (20–40% of labor pool vs. flat $15/hr), backup roster, owner as safety valve

### Risk 3: Customer Acquisition Cost Exceeds Projections
**Mitigation:** Organic channels first (door-to-door, yard signs), referral program ($100 credit for 2 referrals)

### Risk 4: Material Cost Inflation
**Mitigation:** Bulk purchasing (10% discount), multi-supplier strategy, leasing model (lights reused 3+ years)

### Risk 5: Liability Incident (Injury, Property Damage)
**Mitigation:** $1M/$2M insurance, safety training, pre-install photo documentation, signed waivers

### Risk 6: Seasonality (7-Month Revenue Drought)
**Mitigation:** Off-season services (permanent lighting, event décor), maintenance subscriptions, cash reserve (40% of Q4 profits)
