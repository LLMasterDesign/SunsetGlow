## 📝 HOW TO USE THESE TEMPLATES

### Step 1: Choose the Right Option
- **Simple Loan:** Best for conservative investors who want guaranteed repayment.
- **Convertible Note:** Best for investors who believe in long-term growth and want equity upside.
- **Profit Share:** Best for close friends/family who want to support you without long-term commitment.

### Step 2: Fill in the Blanks
- Use a computer (Word, Google Docs, Notes app) or print and handwrite.
- Be specific: Names, dates, dollar amounts.

### Step 3: Sign Digitally or In-Person
- **Digital:** Use phone to sign with finger, save as PDF, email to investor.
- **In-Person:** Print 2 copies, sign both, give 1 to investor, keep 1 for your records.

### Step 4: Store Securely
- Keep a digital copy (Google Drive, Dropbox, email to yourself).
- Keep a physical copy in a folder labeled "Sunset Glow — Investor Agreements."

## 🔒 TRUST-BUILDING TIPS

### For Investors Who Are Hesitant:

**Offer #1: First Right to Profits**
"You get paid back before I pay myself. Every dollar of profit goes to you first until your loan is repaid."

**Offer #2: Personal Guarantee**
"If the business fails, I'll repay you from my personal income over 24 months. You're not at risk."

**Offer #3: Monthly Updates**
"I'll send you a text every month with revenue, jobs completed, and how much closer I am to paying you back."

**Offer #4: Free Install Perk**
"If you invest $1K+, you get a free holiday lighting install every year for life (as long as I'm in business)."
