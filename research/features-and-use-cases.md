# Features and Use-Cases to Capture Attention and Convert

## Attention (above the fold)
- Hero video loop (6–10s) of best nighttime installs; overlay CTA.
- Animated headline with subtle shimmer or twinkle effect.
- Trust bar with review score, insured/licensed badges, and years in business.

## Engagement (scroll journey)
- Before/after slider for rooflines, trees, and yard installs.
- Filterable gallery (Residential, Commercial, Events) with short video clips.
- Scrollytelling “Our Process” (Design → Install → Maintenance → Takedown/Storage).
- Interactive theme picker (e.g., Warm White, Classic Color, Candy Cane, Snowfall).
- Service area checker (zip-based) with instant response and contact options.

## Conversion (lead funnel)
- Smart quote estimator (home type, linear feet estimate, target budget range).
- Sticky CTA: “Get a Free Design” and “Check Availability”.
- Scheduler embed (Calendly/Cal.com) with time slots and service area pre-check.
- Exit-intent modal offering early-bird discount or priority scheduling.

## Retention & Social Proof
- Video testimonials; carousel with names, neighborhood, install type.
- UGC upload (optional): clients share night photos → gallery approval flow.
- Badges: Neighborhoods served, HOA-compliant installs, safety certifications.

## Commercial & Events
- Case studies (foot traffic increase, dwell time, social impressions).
- Spec sheets for RGB trees, tunnels, synchronized shows (PDF download gated).
- Request proposal flow with venue details and seasonal calendar.

## Performance & Accessibility
- Prefer MP4/WebM with bitrate targets; image fallbacks.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`; provide “pause motion” control.
- Lazy-load offscreen media; prefetch hero assets.

## Integrations
- Analytics: GA4 + privacy-first alternative; event tracking on key CTAs.
- CRM/Forms: Airtable/HubSpot/Notion forms; spam protection.
- Payments (optional): deposit link/invoice with Stripe.
