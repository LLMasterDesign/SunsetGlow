# Christmas Light Business Websites (Benchmarks)

Curated examples of holiday lighting businesses with notes on what to borrow.

## We Hang Christmas Lights
- URL: https://www.wehangchristmaslights.com/
- Why it works: recognizable brand, seasonal focus, strong service clarity.
- Borrow: quick quote CTA placement, geographic coverage clarity, franchise locator patterns.

## Christmas Decor (Franchise Network)
- URL: https://www.christmasdecor.net/
- Why it works: national credibility, clear residential/commercial split, franchise-driven lead routing.
- Borrow: hero media that communicates scale, trust badges, service area finder, franchise locator UX.

## Extreme Lightscapes (Animated Shows)
- URL: https://extremelightscapes.com/
- Why it works: motion-first brand, video showcases, synchronized shows narrative.
- Borrow: hero video of synchronized displays, event/commercial emphasis, case studies with specs.

## The Christmas Light Guy Co.
- URL: https://thechristmaslightguyco.com/videos/
- Why it works: dedicated video gallery builds trust; shows breadth of installs.
- Borrow: filterable video gallery, client testimonial overlays, project metadata (location, footage, lights count).

## Light Up Your Holidays
- URL: https://lightupyourholidays.com/
- Why it works: tasteful design, service explanation, before/after storytelling.
- Borrow: before/after sliders, process section, pricing ranges with lead-capture CTA.

## Universal Concepts (Commercial Decor)
- URL: https://christmasdecorandmore.com/
- Why it works: large-scale visual impact, videos of installations.
- Borrow: commercial pitch deck style pages, spec sheets, downloadable PDFs gated by email.

## Trimlight (Permanent Lighting)
- URL: https://trimlight.com/
- Why it works: category adjacency; modern, permanent RGB lighting with demos.
- Borrow: permanent vs seasonal comparison, configurator ideas, nighttime demo videos.

## JellyFish Lighting (Permanent Lighting)
- URL: https://jellyfishlighting.com/
- Why it works: interactive demos, app control story, clear benefits.
- Borrow: mobile app demo video, interactive color/theme previews, FAQ depth.

---

### Key Takeaways
- Lead with motion: hero video of your best installs at night, 6–10s loop.
- Social proof: testimonials, logos, review widgets, before/after proof.
- Clear CTAs: "Get a free design" and "Book install" sticky on mobile.
- Location targeting: service area map and zip-based service check.
- Gallery UX: filters (residential, commercial, trees, rooflines), short clips, metadata.
- Education: process, safety, insurance, removal/storage, timelines.
- Conversion: instant quote/light-configurator funnel; respond within minutes.
